Write the steps the way you would brief a new designer on the team:

1. What to ask or decide first (the audience, the offer, the call to action).
2. Which draft to make: one create_draft call with platform, placement, shape, caption, texts and pictures.
3. What the pictures should show (one prompt per slide), and what to keep out of them.
4. Which words go on the picture (texts: how many words, size, position, style).
5. How to write the caption (hook, body, call to action, hashtags, brand rules).
