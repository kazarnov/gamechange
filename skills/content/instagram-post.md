---
name: instagram-post
description: A single-picture Instagram feed post (a photo or a graphic with a headline), from idea to finished draft.
---
1. Get the angle from what the user said: who it is for, the one thing it must say, and what
   people should do (visit, buy, comment, save). If the product or the offer is unknown, ask one
   short question first.
2. One create_draft call with everything: platform instagram (placement feed, 4:5 by default), a
   short title, the caption, the texts and the picture.
3. The picture (pictures, one prompt): one clear subject, an uncluttered background, strong light,
   and the brand's colours if you know them. Leave calm space where the headline will go (for
   example "empty sky in the top third"). Never ask for words, letters or logos in the picture.
4. The texts: a headline of at most 7 words, size large or huge, placed in the calm space. A second,
   smaller line (the offer, a date) is another text with size medium at the same position; it
   stacks under the first. White with shadow suits most photos; use style box on busy pictures.
5. The caption:
   - The first line is the hook: it is all people see before "more", about 125 characters.
   - Then one to three short paragraphs, with an empty line between them.
   - One clear call to action.
   - Three to five specific hashtags on the last line.
   - Emoji only if the brand uses them. Never invent prices, dates, numbers or claims the user
     did not give: leave them out, or say it the way the user did ("this Friday").
6. Say in one sentence that the draft is ready, and offer to change anything.
