---
name: instagram-story
description: A full-screen 9:16 Instagram story (an announcement, offer, countdown or behind-the-scenes moment).
---
1. One create_draft call: platform instagram, placement story, a title, the texts and the picture.
   A story has no caption: every word goes on the picture.
2. The picture: a vertical scene with the subject in the middle third, and calm areas at the top
   and bottom for text.
3. Texts: Instagram's own buttons cover the top and bottom of a story; positions top and bottom
   already keep clear of them. Use one headline (huge or large) and one short line saying what to
   do ("Link in bio", "Reply with SALE", "Tap the sticker"). Style box reads best on photos.
4. Say it is ready, and offer changes.
