---
name: instagram-carousel
description: An Instagram carousel (2 to 10 slides) that teaches, lists, compares or tells a story, with words on every slide.
---
1. Plan four to seven slides:
   - Slide 1 is the hook: a promise or a bold claim that makes people swipe.
   - One idea per middle slide.
   - The last slide is the call to action: save, share, follow, or visit.
2. One create_draft call with everything: platform instagram, placement feed (all slides share
   one shape, 4:5), title, caption, the texts for every slide, and one picture prompt per slide.
3. Pictures: keep the look the same by repeating the same style words (palette, light, setting)
   in every prompt and changing only the subject. A text-only slide needs no picture: give an
   empty prompt for it, and it gets the draft's plain background (background colours it).
4. Texts on every slide (slide set on each). Slide 1 gets the headline (huge). Middle slides get a
   short title (large) and, if needed, up to 20 words of detail (medium) at the same position. Use
   the same font, colour and position on every slide so the carousel reads as one piece.
5. Caption: a hook on the first line, one line on what the carousel gives, the call to action
   ("Save this for later"), and three to five hashtags on the last line.
6. Say how many slides it has, and offer changes.
