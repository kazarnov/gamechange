---
name: manage-todos
description: Complete, delete, filter or read back items in the TodoMVC todo list.
---
- To read the list: the page text shows every item and "N items left". Report them.
- To mark an item done: click the "Toggle Todo" checkbox in that item's row. Clicking it
  again un-marks it.
- To delete one item: hover the "Toggle Todo" checkbox in that item's row; a "Delete" button
  then appears in the same row. Click it.
- To remove all finished items: click "Clear completed".
- To filter: click the "All", "Active" or "Completed" links at the bottom.
- After changing something, check the page shows the result before you finish.
